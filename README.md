The GPS's one-line excuse for sending the driver down the wrong road, written in its own smug, defensive voice — it swears it was a shortcut, blames the map, and reveals the lake was the road all along.

Entry: "It was a shortcut. The map said turn left at the lake, it just never said the lake was the road."
